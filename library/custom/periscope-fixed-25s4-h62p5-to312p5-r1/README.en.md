# 25-S4 R3 two-mirror periscope

[中文](README.md)

The two mirror stations each use a 12.5 mm base, a ZZ-13-M1 adapter and an MT-FLF1S fixed mount. Their nominal heights are 62.5 mm and 312.5 mm. The 45 mm-wide base has four mounting holes on a 50 × 25 mm pattern. On a 25 mm grid the optical axis is half a pitch across the grid.
